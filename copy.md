# LEAFO - Website Copy v1

**Tone:** editorial, restrained, gallery-like. Sentences are short. No exclamation marks except where flagged. No corporate filler. No emoji. British/Indian English spelling (colour, programme, optimise).

**Voice rules:**
- Lead with the object, not the brand.
- Specifics over adjectives. "FRP, 4 finishes, 9 sizes" beats "premium, beautifully crafted, world-class."
- Never use: "we believe", "our journey", "passion", "perfffect", "let's grow something meaningful", or any anthropomorphic plant-talk.
- One word the brand owns: **Quiet.** Use sparingly.

## 5. Finishes page (`/finishes`)

**Page title:** Finishes

**Intro:**
> Every LEAFO planter is finished by hand. Five surface treatments, each engineered for a different light, climate, and visual register. Specified at order, applied in our Anand workshop.

### Gloss
High-sheen automotive paint. Holds colour under direct sun and rain, reads as enamel. Best for interiors and shaded outdoor spaces where reflection is part of the design.

### Matte
A flat, non-reflective finish. Sits quietly in a room. Pairs well with raw materials: stone, wood, linen, brushed metal.

### Rustic
A hand-worked surface with tonal depth and visible texture. No two pieces are identical. Reads as ceramic, weighs a fraction of it.

### Stone
A mineral finish that imitates carved stone. Suited to landscape, entry courtyards, and hospitality projects where the planter should disappear into the architecture.

### Orange Peel
A subtle dimpled texture, named for its surface pattern. Catches light without reflecting it. Best at human scale, where the surface can be touched.

## 7. About page (`/about`)

### Hero
**Eyebrow:** About

**Headline:**

### Story (two short blocks)

**Block 1 - Origin**
> LEAFO is a planter manufacturer based in Anand, Gujarat. We make fiber pots, full-size planters, and modular fiber systems for homes, commercial spaces, and landscape projects.

> The company started with a simple observation: India had hundreds of pot manufacturers, but very few thinking about planters as designed objects. Most catalogues offered shape, but not silhouette. Colour, but not finish. We started LEAFO to close that gap.

**Block 2 - Approach**
> Every series in the catalogue is drawn before it's moulded. Sizes are chosen so that a single design works at desktop scale and architectural scale. Finishes are tested for outdoor weather over multiple seasons. Production happens in our own facility, with hand-laying, hand-finishing, and quality control done in-house.

### What we make (summary block)
- 12 collections, 60+ size variants
- 5 finishes: gloss, matte, rustic, stone, orange peel
- Material: fiber-reinforced plastic (FRP)
- Built for indoor and outdoor use
- Shipped across India, exported on request
